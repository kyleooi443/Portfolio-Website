## Why I started

RECLAIM explores what could happen if passing on an unused item felt simpler and more worthwhile. The concept combines a marketplace with assistance for describing and presenting items.

## The idea and constraints

Any AI assistance should make a listing clearer while keeping the seller in control of what is accurate. Trust, straightforward browsing, and the practical work of an exchange are central design questions.
