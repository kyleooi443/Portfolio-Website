## Why I started

The idea behind NEXTWISE begins with a familiar question: when everything feels important, how do you decide what deserves attention next? This concept explores a companion that helps make that choice more deliberate.

## The idea and constraints

The challenge is to suggest a meaningful next action without turning productivity into another stream of notifications. A useful version would need to be calm, understandable, and easy to correct when a suggestion misses the mark.
