## Constraints make choices visible

*Sample article — replace this draft with Kyle’s own writing before publishing.*

When time, space, or technology is limited, every choice has a job. Ask what someone needs to notice first, what they need to do next, and which details can wait.

## A small checklist

- Is the most important information easy to find?
- Can someone use it with a keyboard and on a phone?
- Does each visual detail help explain the idea?

The answer will not always be perfect. A clear reason for a design choice makes it easier to revisit later.
